# Institutional brand asset

`rio-pomba-valley.png` is the original, unmodified Rio Pomba Valley logo (291 × 244), obtained from the regional institutional partner Polo Audiovisual da Zona da Mata on 2026-09-16.

- Source page: https://www.poloaudiovisual.org.br/rio-pomba-valley-criatividade-conhecimento-e-inovacao/
- Original asset: https://www.poloaudiovisual.org.br/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/Rio-POmba-Valley.png

Display at its original aspect ratio on a white plate. Do not recolor, trace, distort, or apply filters. The separate Talent Valley V mark is a product identity, not a replacement for this institutional logo.

## `talent-valley-email.png`

`talent-valley-email.png` is a transparent, high-density (612 × 300) export of the official Talent Valley geometric mountain mark. Its geometry and palette are copied verbatim from the exact SVG paths in `frontend/src/components/brand/TalentValleyMark.tsx`; it exists only for transactional email headers, where inline SVG is unreliable across email clients. The mark's `currentColor` facet stroke is resolved to `#F4F7F6` for the dark charcoal email background. If the component paths change, regenerate this asset from the component instead of editing the PNG directly.
